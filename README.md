# Qwerty in Motion Launcher

Launcher for our replication of Nicolau and Jorge (2012), thumb typing while walking.

- Launcher: https://rkentmc.github.io/qwerty-in-motion
- Data form (no sign-in): https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/FHSWm1Jixy

## Who holds what

- **Participant's iPhone:** the launcher and Monkeytype, both in Safari.
- **Experimenter's phone:** metronome, camera, and the data form saved to the Home Screen.

## Before the first round

1. On the participant's iPhone, open the launcher in Safari (type the address above or scan a QR code of it).
2. Tap **Copy settings**. Open Monkeytype, go to Settings, find **import/export settings**, tap **import**, and paste. This turns off backspace and keeps the test going after a mistake. Do this once per phone.
3. On your phone, open the form, choose **New Participant**, and fill it in. The participant ID is P, your teammate number, then the participant number, for example `P-4-07`. Never a name.
4. Run **Practice 1 to 3**, one phrase each: sitting, slow walking, normal walking. Practice is not recorded.

## Each round

There are 9 rounds. Round 1 on the launcher is the first run of the session, whatever its movement and grip.

1. Read this round's movement and grip from the participant's order sheet.
2. Warm-up: tap the round's **Warm-up** button and have the participant type its 2 phrases. Warm-ups are not recorded.
3. For a walking round, start the metronome: 78 for slow walking, 120 for normal walking.
4. Tap the round's **Test** button on the participant's iPhone and hand it over. The participant types the 5 phrases.
5. Take the phone back and photograph the Monkeytype results screen with your phone. Check the photo is readable before the next round.

The launcher ticks each button once tapped and marks the round done.

## Upload the photos

Upload the results photos by hand to the team's SharePoint results folder. Name each one with the participant ID and round, for example `P-4-07_round8.jpg`.

## Fill the form

After the session, submit the form once per round from the uploaded photos, choosing **Round results**.

- From the order sheet: participant ID, round, movement, grip.
- From the photo: wpm, raw, acc, correct, incorrect, extra, missed.
- From your notes during the session: walking errors (0 if none), whether a photo was taken, and anything else worth recording.

## Next participant

On the launcher, tap **Reset ticks**, then **Yes, clear all ticks**.
